Our story

Built for people who live across borders

NovaPay started in 2019 when our founders, between them holding four passports, got tired of paying to move their own money. We began with one product, a card that charged the real exchange rate, and grew it into a full account.

Today 3.4 million people bank with us across 28 markets. We hold an e-money licence, safeguard customer balances at a tier-one partner bank and make our money from subscriptions rather than from hidden spreads.

Security and licensing

Regulated, safeguarded, and boring about it

Your balance is not lent out. It sits in a safeguarded account at a partner bank, separate from company money, so it is still yours whatever happens to us. Everything else is the unglamorous work: independent audits, penetration tests and 24-hour fraud monitoring.

  • E-money licence, with customer funds safeguarded, not lent
  • Card freezing, per-merchant limits and instant virtual cards
  • Every payment approved with biometrics on your device
  • Fraud monitored around the clock, with refunds inside five days
