That was your mortgage payment

A missed mortgage payment is reported to credit reference agencies and can affect your ability to borrow for six years. If cancelling was deliberate, ignore this.

If money is tight this month, a payment holiday or a change of payment date is usually arranged in one phone call, and neither is recorded as a missed payment.
