Security and protection

Your money is protected, and so are you

Eligible deposits are protected up to £85,000 per person by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme, and up to £170,000 for a joint account. Beyond that statutory protection, we refund customers who lose money to authorised push payment fraud.

  • FSCS protection up to £85,000, or £170,000 jointly
  • Card freezing, spending limits and trusted-payee lists in the app
  • Every payment confirmed with biometrics or a card reader
  • Reimbursement for authorised push payment fraud

Our history

A hundred and fifty years of the same job

Meridian opened in 1874 as a savings bank for shipyard workers who had nowhere safe to keep their wages. The business has changed a great deal since; what we are actually for has not.

We serve 2.1 million personal customers and 84,000 businesses from 214 branches. We are funded by customer deposits rather than wholesale markets, which is unfashionable and has served our customers well through three downturns.
